Help & Methodology

Purpose. Estimate ROI of a feed additive in commercial layers by combining: production uplift (HDP), sellable quality uplift (% sellable), and benefits from improved liveability (mortality reduction).

Inputs
  • Flock size (hens). Default is 1 for unit testing; set the actual flock for production.
  • Phase length (weeks). Weeks analyzed (typically 60).
  • Feed intake / hen / day (g). Average grams per hen per day.
  • Feed additive inv. (€/t). Incremental cost per tonne of feed when additive is used.
  • Average laying rate (HDP, %). Mean hen-day %.
  • Egg price (€/egg). Average selling price.
Effects
  • Mortality reduction (pp). Reduction in mortality across the phase, expressed in percentage points of the baseline phase mortality.
  • Increment of laying eggs (%). Additional HDP.
  • Increment of sellable eggs (%). Additional percentage of eggs that are sellable.
Methodology: Mortality & Liveability

Baseline deaths = flock × (baseline mortality over phase ÷ 100). Saved hens = flock × (min(mortality reduction pp, baseline mortality) ÷ 100).

  1. Curve (Lohmann) model. Deaths are distributed weekly using a Lohmann cumulative mortality curve. We convert cumulative to weekly incidence and then:
    • Saved eggs = Σweeks(saved hens at week) × (HDPwith/100 × 7) × (weeks remaining).
    • Feed reduction = Σweeks(deaths at week) × (feed/hen/day in kg) × 7 × (weeks remaining).

Revenue & Costs. Eggs sold = Eggs produced × % sellable. Eggs revenue = Eggs sold × Egg price. Additive cost = Feed with additive (t) × Additive €/t. Net benefit = Eggs revenue − Additive investment. Diff net benefit = With net benefit − Without net benefit.

ROI. ROI = Diff net benefit ÷ Additive cost. Break‑even occurs when ROI = 0 (net benefit = 0). ROI = 1 means a 100% return (net benefit equals additive cost).
